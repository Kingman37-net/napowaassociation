# NAPOWA Association

**National Police Wives Welfare Association (NAPOWA) — Member Association** connecting police wives, widows, and families across Kenya through leadership, welfare programs, and community support.

🌐 **Live site:** [www.napowaassociation.co.ke](https://www.napowaassociation.co.ke)

---

## About the Association

The NAPOWA Association is the member-facing arm of the National Police Wives Welfare Association. It provides a structured platform for members to:

- Join and participate in the national welfare network
- Access welfare, counseling, and empowerment programs
- Register widows and orphans for support
- Track contributions and welfare requests through the member portal
- Connect with leadership and branch coordinators

---

## Programs & Services

- 💼 **Training & Empowerment** — vocational and income-generating skills
- 💙 **Welfare & Counseling** — psychosocial support for families
- 🤝 **Community Outreach** — advocacy and social engagement
- 👩‍👧 **Widows & Orphans** — registration and dedicated care programs
- 🌱 **Membership Portal** — digital dashboard for members
