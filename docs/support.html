How We Support Police Families

Five pillars of care – from active duty to retirement

Housing

Assisting police families with safe, affordable housing solutions, home improvement grants, and rent subsidies for widows.

Education

Scholarships, school fees support, and mentorship programs for children of police officers – ensuring no child is left behind.

Health Care

Access to medical check-ups, mental health counseling, and hospital fee assistance for police families and retired officers.

Injured Officers

Immediate financial aid, rehabilitation support, and family care packages for officers wounded in the line of duty.

Retired Police

Pension advocacy, social events, welfare programs, and health support for retired officers and their spouses.
