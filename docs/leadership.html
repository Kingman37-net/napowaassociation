National Leadership Team

The National Police Wives Welfare Association (NAPOWA) is guided by a dedicated leadership team committed to empowerment, welfare, advocacy, and sustainable support for police families across Kenya.

Jenetrix Aoko Orwa Otieno

Founder & CEO / National Coordinator

Provides national leadership and strategic oversight of NAPOWA, championing empowerment, advocacy, and welfare programs for police families across Kenya.

Anne Auma Owiti Ochieng

National Welfare Lead

Oversees national welfare programs, governance, and coordination of regional branches to ensure effective program delivery.

Maureen Kamaria

Secretary General

Oversees daily administrative operations, official correspondence, meetings, and implementation of organizational policies.

Josephine Kirathe

National Vice Secretary General & Rongai Coordinator

Doreen Kithia Kathure

National Vice Representative

Evelyn Mutunga

Nairobi Representative & Branch Secretary

Beryl Achieng Otieno

Director of Finance

Responsible for financial planning, budgeting, accountability, and transparency of NAPOWA resources.

Grace Kabubu Osur

National Information Officer

Oversees digital communication, publicity strategies, and dissemination of official association information.

Veronica Okinda
