Police Widows & Orphans Program

Restoring dignity, stability, and hope to families of fallen police officers

About the Program

The NAPOWA Police Widows & Orphans Program supports widows and children of deceased police officers through welfare assistance, education support, counseling, and empowerment initiatives.

🎓 Education Support

School fees, uniforms, books, and academic follow-up.

💊 Medical & Counseling

Medical assistance and psychosocial support services.

🚨 Emergency Relief

Rapid response to crisis situations and emergencies.

💼 Widow Empowerment

Skills training and income-generating opportunities.

NAPOWA
