Training & Empowerment

Building sustainable livelihoods for police families

Program Overview

NAPOWA’s Training & Empowerment Program equips police wives, widows, and families with practical skills to achieve economic independence and long-term stability.

Key Focus Areas

  • Entrepreneurship & small business training
  • Financial literacy & savings education
  • Vocational skills development
  • Mentorship and peer support

Impact

Through this program, beneficiaries gain confidence, income-generating skills, and the ability to support their families sustainably.
