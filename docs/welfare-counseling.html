Welfare & Counseling

Supporting emotional, social, and mental well-being

Program Overview

This program provides holistic welfare and counseling support to police families experiencing trauma, loss, stress, or crisis.

Services Offered

  • Psychosocial counseling
  • Medical assistance referrals
  • Emergency welfare support
  • Grief and trauma counseling

Our Commitment

NAPOWA is committed to restoring dignity, hope, and emotional resilience among police families nationwide.

NAPOWA

National Police Wives Welfare Association — empowering police families, widows, and orphans across Kenya since 2014.
