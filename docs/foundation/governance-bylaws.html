Governance & Bylaws

NAPOWA Foundation is committed to ethical leadership, transparency, accountability, and responsible management in all its operations.

Governance Structure

General Assembly

  • Supreme decision-making body of the Foundation
  • Composed of all registered members
  • Approves policies, annual plans, and financial reports

Steering Committee

  • Responsible for strategic leadership and oversight
  • Implements decisions of the General Assembly
  • Ensures compliance with the Constitution and Bylaws

Sub-Committees

  • Finance & Audit Committee
  • Programs & Welfare Committee
  • Membership & Ethics Committee
  • Ad-hoc committees as required

Bylaws (Articles)

Article 1: Name & Legal Status

The organization shall be known as NAPOWA Foundation, operating as a not-for-profit entity under the registration of NAPOWA Association.

Article 2: Vision & Mission

To empower police families and communities through welfare, education, economic empowerment, and social support programs.

Article 3: Membership

  • Membership shall be open to eligible individuals and families
  • Members shall pay a commitment fee as determined by the Assembly

Article 4: Leadership & Elections

  • Leaders shall be elected democratically
  • Terms of office shall be defined by policy

Article 5: Meetings

  • Annual General Meeting held once per year
  • Special meetings may be convened when necessary

Article 6: Financial Management

  • Funds used strictly for Foundation objectives
  • Annual budgets and audited financial reports required

Article 7: Transparency & Accountability

The Foundation commits to open reporting, ethical conduct, and donor accountability.

Article 8: Conflict of Interest

Leaders and members must disclose conflicts and act in the best interest of the Foundation.

Article 9: Dispute Resolution

Internal disputes shall be resolved amicably through mediation and internal mechanisms.

Article 10: Amendments

Amendments to the bylaws shall be approved by the General Assembly.

Article 11: Dissolution

Upon dissolution, assets shall be transferred to a similar not-for-profit organization.

Article 12: Adoption

These bylaws are adopted and shall take effect upon approval by the General Assembly.
