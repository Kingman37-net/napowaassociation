Beneficiary Eligibility

NAPOWA Foundation ensures that support reaches those who truly need it. Here’s a clear guide on who qualifies as a beneficiary.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be a registered member of NAPOWA Foundation
  • Must have paid the one-time commitment fee of KSh 10
  • Must be a resident of Kenya
  • Must demonstrate genuine need for financial, educational, or empowerment support
  • Must actively participate in Foundation activities or community service

How to Apply as a Beneficiary

  • Ensure your membership registration is complete
  • Contact the Foundation via WhatsApp, email, or office
  • Provide necessary documentation to verify eligibility
  • Participate in any assessment required by the Foundation
  • Approved beneficiaries will receive support according to Foundation programs
