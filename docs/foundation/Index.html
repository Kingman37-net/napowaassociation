Who We Are

NAPOWA Foundation is a non-profit, community-focused organization established by the Executives of the National Police Wives Welfare Association. We exist to support police families and the communities they serve through empowerment, welfare, education, and sustainable development initiatives.

Our Mission

To unite police families and community members through dignity, trust, empowerment, and inclusive programs that promote social welfare, financial inclusion, mental well-being, and sustainable livelihoods.

Our Key Programs

Membership & Belonging

Creating a strong, inclusive network for police families and communities.

Savings & Affordable Loans

Promoting collective savings and access to interest-free financial support.

Welfare & Emergency Support

Providing timely assistance to families facing hardship or emergencies.

Empowerment & Skills Training

Building self-reliance through skills development and capacity building.

Scholarships & Education

Supporting education through scholarships, mentorship, and guidance.

Mental Health & Counseling

Promoting emotional well-being through counseling and psychosocial support.
