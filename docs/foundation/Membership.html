Membership

Join NAPOWA Foundation and be part of a stronger, united, and empowered community. Membership is simple, accessible, and opens doors to support, skills, and community programs.

Who Can Join

Membership is open to all women, men, and families who:

  • Support the objectives of NAPOWA Foundation
  • Commit to positive community engagement
  • Are willing to participate in Foundation programs and activities

Commitment Fee

Each member contributes a one-time commitment fee of KSh 10. This small fee helps sustain our programs and ensures every member is invested in the community.

Member Responsibilities

  • Actively participate in Foundation activities
  • Promote the Foundation’s values of dignity, trust, and empowerment
  • Recruit new members to strengthen the community network
  • Ensure timely payment of fees and contributions
  • Uphold ethical conduct at all times

Benefits of Membership

  • Access to welfare and emergency support
  • Participation in savings and interest-free loan programs
  • Eligibility for scholarships, mentorship, and training
  • Connection to a network of empowered police families and communities
  • Opportunities for personal growth and skills development

Join NAPOWA Foundation

Ready to become a member? Reach out via WhatsApp, email, or our contact form. Your journey to empowerment and community impact begins today.
