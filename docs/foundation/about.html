About NAPOWA Foundation

Serving communities with dignity by strengthening police families and building trust between the police and the public.

Who We Are

Our Background

NAPOWA Foundation is a community-focused, non-profit organization established by the Executives of the National Police Wives Welfare Association (NAPOWA). The Foundation was formed to respond to the social, economic, and emotional needs of police families and the communities they serve.

Through inclusive programs and collaborative partnerships, NAPOWA Foundation works to promote dignity, trust, empowerment, and sustainable community development.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To build empowered, self-reliant, and cohesive communities founded on dignity, trust, and mutual respect.

Our Mission

To unite police families and community members through empowerment initiatives that promote social welfare, education, financial inclusion, mental well-being, and sustainable livelihoods.

Our Core Values

Dignity

We uphold respect, fairness, and human worth in all our work.

Trust

We promote honesty, transparency, and accountability.

Empowerment

We strengthen individuals and communities to become self-reliant.

Our Commitment

  • Strengthening community–police relations
  • Supporting sustainable livelihoods
  • Creating measurable and lasting social impact
