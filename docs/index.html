National Police Wives Welfare Association

Empowering police wives, widows, orphans, and families across Kenya through welfare support, counseling, advocacy, and sustainable empowerment programs.

Support Our Work

Who We Are

NAPOWA is a registered non-profit organization founded in 2014 under the Societies Act of Kenya. The association exists to uplift and support police families by addressing social, economic, and psychological challenges faced by officers’ families across the country.

Our Core Focus Areas

💙

Welfare & Counseling

Psychosocial and emotional support for police families.

💼

Economic Empowerment

Skills training and income-generating initiatives.

👩‍👧

Widows & Orphans

Dedicated care and protection programs.

🤝

Community Outreach

Advocacy, mentorship, and social engagement.
