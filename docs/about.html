About NAPOWA

Building strength, unity, and resilience among police families across Kenya

Who We Are

The National Police Wives Welfare Association (NAPOWA) is a registered non-profit organization founded in 2014 under the Societies Act of Kenya. The association was established to address the unique social, economic, and emotional challenges faced by police wives, widows, orphans, and families of serving and fallen officers.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To empower police families through welfare support, counseling, advocacy, and sustainable economic programs that enhance dignity, stability, and resilience.

🌍 Our Vision

A united and empowered police family community that is socially, economically, and emotionally secure across Kenya.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity and accountability
  • Compassion and inclusivity
  • Transparency and trust
  • Service and community impact
  • Unity and collaboration

Our Background

Since its establishment, NAPOWA has grown into a nationally coordinated association with branch representation in various regions. Through strong leadership and partnerships, the association continues to implement impactful programs that support welfare, economic empowerment, and community outreach for police families.
